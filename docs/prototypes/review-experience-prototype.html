BrainIA-Mobile · Review experience prototype · Oct 2026

Review in a minute, one tap at a time.

Review today is a data-entry form: pick an item, choose a friendly name, set a canonical label, open a category sheet, mark equivalent entities, pick roles, then confirm a dialog. This prototype turns it into a short, focused session: one plain question per card, the likely answer ready to accept with one tap or a swipe, undo instead of confirm dialogs, repetitive cases batched, and the obvious ones handled for you.
