# Sub-screen design audit A: entity, email, event

Task `task_9d897bc21ac6`, branch `LatinGeek/glass-design-polish`. This is a read-only audit. No source files were edited, nothing was committed or stashed, and no device or adb was used.

**Screens in scope:** `app/entity/[id].tsx`, `app/email/[id].tsx` and `app/event/[id].tsx`.

**Components read** because these screens render them: `Screen`/`Section`, `GlassSurface`, `GlassPressable`, `BottomSheet`, `StateView`, `Row`/`IconTile`, `Timeline`, `Graph` (preview mode), `SignalCard`, `CorrectionPanel`, and from `controls`: `Button`, `Chip`, `Tag`, `Field` and `SegmentedControl`. I also read `lib/themes.ts` and `useAsyncResource` in `lib/hooks.ts`.

**Rules applied:**
- Use the 20dp page gutter.
- Use the `theme.spacing` scale (4/8/12/16/24/32/40) and the radius scale (10/14/20/28).
- Every card goes through `GlassSurface`.
- Touch targets are at least 44dp (`layout.minTarget`), or reach 44 with `hitSlop`.
- Copy is in Spanish, except proper nouns.
- Motion comes from the theme tokens and is gated by Reduce Motion.

**Line numbers.** Screen and component line numbers are from the working tree at audit time. `components/layout/Screen.tsx` was being edited by another process during the audit, so its line numbers may shift.

**Severity:**
- **High:** visibly broken, misleading, or the feature is hard to use.
- **Medium:** a clear system-rule violation the user will notice.
- **Low:** polish.
